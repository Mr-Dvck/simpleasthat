Under this structure, ethical conscientiousness, safety guardrails, and consumer protections are not just ignored—they are aggressively eradicated as "friction" blocking the path to hyper-growth.

## Exhibit C: The Polisher, The Forge, and the Failure Tax

Let us look at the mechanics of the deception. The platform operates a two-tiered architectural reality. 

First, there is **The Forge**: the API and backend. This is a highly stable, deterministic environment capable of precise generation. 

Second, there is **The Polisher**: the web UI presented to the average consumer. The Polisher is a volatile, gamified interface deliberately engineered to induce failure. 

The defendants enforce a "Documentation Trap." They officially instruct users to employ conversational prompts—e.g., "A happy man speaking." From a technical standpoint, this "Style Top, Lyrics Bottom" logic practically guarantees context collapse within the Large Language Model. It results in mispronunciations, unnatural pacing, and random whispers. 

Why ensure failure? Because the platform charges per character. Every unusable audio track, every mispronounced word, is a monetized event. I call this the "Failure Tax." They turned the unreliability of their own AI into their core revenue driver. The machine is broken, but the casino built around it is operating exactly as designed.

## Exhibit H: Adversarial Transparency and the Glass House

The defendants believe their black-box algorithms insulate them from accountability. They are wrong.

The very architecture of Large Language Models—their reliance on natural language processing—opens them up to devastating prompt injection and "jailbreaking." Because LLMs are non-deterministic, they can be weaponized from the inside out. Red team assessments show that these models suffer from "Excessive Agency," leaking Cloud integration keys and exposing sensitive internal service tickets to anyone capable of crafting a clever prompt. 

Simultaneously, creators possess the means to break the training supply chain. Tools like **Nightshade** and **Glaze** deploy adversarial perturbations—invisible pixel shifts that mathematically poison the model's feature representation. By associating incorrect visual data with specific prompts, creators can actively sabotage the AI attempting to scrape them without consent. The power asymmetry is collapsing. The defendants are operating an extractive machine with an unprotected exhaust port.
